DuReTTS: Expressive Dual-Reference Text-to-Speech with a Stochastic Style Bottleneck

Audio samples accompanying our ICASSP submission

1. Cross-Lingual Dubbing

In cross-lingual dubbing, the synthesized speech must speak a different language while preserving the style of the prompt speech. Prompt is the source-language reference; VoxCPM2, IndexTTS2, and our DuReTTS generate the dubbed speech in the target language.

2. Dual-Reference Generation

Given two input references, DuReTTS clones the timbre of the Speaker reference and the style of the Style reference at the same time. IndexTTS2 is included as a single-reference baseline.

Notes

All audios are provided in WAV format. In the cross-lingual dubbing section, Prompt is the source-language speech whose style should be preserved in the target language. In the dual-reference section, Speaker and Style are the two conditioning references providing timbre and style respectively. VoxCPM2 and IndexTTS2 results are generated by their official open-source implementations; DuReTTS is our proposed method.
